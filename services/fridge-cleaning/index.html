Fridge Cleaning

About This Service

A fridge clean is one of those jobs that is easy to put off until the next food shop. The Polished Standard offers focused fridge cleaning to help refresh the space and deal with the everyday spills and residue that collect inside.

It can be a useful clean to arrange before a large shop, ahead of hosting or whenever the fridge needs attention. Tell us about your appliance and its condition so we can discuss the work and the preparation needed.

What Is Included

The cleaning scope is discussed for your fridge, including its interior surfaces, shelving, drawers and areas that need particular attention. We’ll agree what can be included and how the appliance should be prepared. Food handling, freezer compartments and any additional appliance work must be discussed before the appointment.

Our Process

  1. Tell Us About Your Fridge: Share its size, type and the areas you would like help with.
  2. Agree the Details: Discuss the cleaning scope, quote and available appointment.
  3. Prepare the Appliance: Follow the preparation arrangements agreed for food and access.
  4. Enjoy a Fresh Start: The agreed areas are cleaned so you can organise your fridge again.
01. Can I book before Christmas or a large food shop?
+

Yes, you can enquire about a suitable date. Appointments depend on current availability, so share any deadline when you get in touch.

02. Do I need to empty the fridge first?
+

We’ll discuss how it should be prepared before the visit, including arrangements for food and any items inside.

03. Does this include freezer defrosting?
+

Freezer defrosting is not included automatically. Mention any freezer requirements when enquiring so we can discuss what is possible.

04. Can it be combined with another cleaning service?
+

Tell us about the other work you need. We’ll discuss whether the tasks can be arranged together and quote for the agreed scope.
