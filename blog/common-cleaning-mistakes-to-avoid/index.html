What Airbnb Hosts Should Expect From a Turnover Clean

A useful turnover service gives you visibility of your property as well as cleaning it for the next guests. Here is what to discuss before booking.

When you cannot be at the property between every stay, cleaning updates become especially useful. You need to understand how the space has been left, what has been prepared and whether anything needs your attention before the next arrival.

Start with a clear property brief

Share the property address, guest departure and arrival times, and access arrangements. Explain how you want the space presented and which tasks you need considered. Laundry, linen and any additional requirements should be discussed explicitly.

Ask how condition is recorded

The Polished Standard provides before and after videos as part of its Airbnb service. These offer a visual record of the property’s condition and the presentation after cleaning. Agree how the videos will be shared and any areas that should remain private.

Make reporting useful

Our property reports are sent within the allocated cleaning slot and highlight matters such as damage, missing items and low stock. A host can then decide what needs action. Clear reporting arrangements help avoid important details getting lost in a general update.

Agree restocking before it is needed

Essential supplies can be replenished where agreed. Discuss which items are involved, how they will be supplied and any associated costs. Restocking arrangements should be specific to your property and understood before the visit.

Key takeaway: Agree the cleaning, condition reporting and supply arrangements together, so everyone understands the guest preparation brief.
