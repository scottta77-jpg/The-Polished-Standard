Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know Before Booking

What services does HousEase offer?

HousEase offers regular home cleaning, deep cleaning, kitchen and bathroom cleaning, carpet and upholstery care, and move-in or move-out cleaning.

How do I book a service?

Choose your cleaning service, pick a date and time that suits you, and confirm online or by phone. You'll receive a booking confirmation right away.

Can I choose a specific service time?

Yes. Select your preferred date and time slot when booking, and your cleaner will arrive within the window you choose.

Are HousEase professionals verified?

Yes. Every cleaner is background-checked, trained in our cleaning standards, and fully insured before joining the HousEase team.

What is included in the service price?

Your price covers the cleaner's time, professional-grade supplies and equipment, and a final quality check. No hidden fees are added later.

Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

Yes. You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment, straight from your booking confirmation.

Need Help Choosing a Service?

Our team is here to help with bookings, services, pricing, and any questions you may have.
