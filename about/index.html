Your Questions

A Few Things Before We Clean

What cleaning services do you offer?

We offer cleaning for homes, commercial properties and Airbnbs, alongside focused kitchen, bathroom and fridge cleaning. Tell us what you need so we can discuss the right service.

Which areas do you cover?

Our local service covers Elstree, Borehamwood and Radlett. Airbnb cleaning is currently focused on Borehamwood. If you are nearby, send your postcode so we can check coverage for your service.

How do I arrange a clean?

Send an enquiry with your location, property details and the type of cleaning you need. We’ll discuss the work and confirm availability before a booking is agreed.

Do you supply cleaning products?

Yes, we supply our own professional cleaning products. Please tell us about delicate finishes, product preferences or anything we should know before the visit.

Are you insured?

Yes. The Polished Standard is insured up to £2 million. If your business or property manager needs further details, please ask when enquiring.

How much does cleaning cost?

The quote depends on the property, the work involved and the frequency you need. Send a few details so we can discuss the scope and price with you.

Not Sure Which Service Fits?

Tell us about your space and the areas you would like help with. We’ll talk through the options.
